SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made on March 3, 2026, between
Bluefern Supplies Ltd. ("Provider"), represented by Pat Example, and
Harbor Lane Bakery Inc. ("Client"), represented by Sam Placeholder.

1. Services
Provider will deliver baking ingredients and packaging to Client every
week, as listed in Schedule A.

2. Fees and Payment
Client will pay Provider $4,500 per month. Client pays the
full amount due upfront for the first month. Later invoices are payable
Net 90.
Late payments incur a fee of 5% per month.

3. Term and Renewal
This Agreement starts on April 1, 2026, and lasts 12 months. It
automatically renews for another 12 months unless either party gives
written notice of 15 days before the end of the current term.

4. Termination
Client may end this Agreement early only by paying a cancellation fee
equal to six months of fees.

5. Liability
Client accepts unlimited liability for any loss, damage, or claim
arising from its use of the delivered goods. Provider's liability is
limited to the fees paid in the previous month.

Both parties will keep the terms of this Agreement confidential.
